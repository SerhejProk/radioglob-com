Frequently asked questions

What is the best radio for sleep?

Nature sounds and ambient music without sudden changes. Start with the stations at the top of the list and pick one with no voice and no drums.

Can I listen on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in your phone's browser and tap a station. You can also add the radio to your home screen.
