About RadioGlob

RadioGlob is a free internet radio that runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer. Pick a collection or a country, press play, and listen: no sign-up, nothing to install.

▶ Open the player

What it does

  • Thousands of live stations from the open radio-browser.info catalogue, in genre and mood collections (jazz, classical, chill, K-pop, news, kids, language learning and more) and by country
  • A “Station of the Day” on the home page
  • A sleep timer (15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes) with a gentle fade-out, and an ambience panel
  • Favourites and a song notebook: tap the heart while a track is playing to save the song and the station
  • Installs like an app on phone and desktop, and the now-playing text scrolls in the window title
  • 12 visual themes and an English, French and Russian interface
  • A separate audio mixer: load your own tracks, trim, fade, equalize, add echo, and export to WAV

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy and no account to create.

Where do the stations come from?

From the open radio-browser.info community catalogue. A station plays only if its stream is secure (https), because browsers block insecure streams on a secure site. A small number of stations are intentionally not listed.

What do you store about me?

Your favourites, song notebook, volume and settings stay in your own browser on your own device. The mixer processes your files in the browser: they are never uploaded. To count visits, the site uses GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal data) and Yandex Metrica (which uses cookies).

A station does not play or is missing. What now?

Write to support@radioglob.com with the station name. Streams change or go offline often, and we check what is reported.
